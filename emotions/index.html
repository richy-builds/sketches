Where the atlas comes from

  • Robert Plutchik, Emotion: A Psychoevolutionary Synthesis (1980): the wheel, the intensities, the dyads and the eight adaptive jobs.
  • James Russell, "A circumplex model of affect", J. Personality & Social Psychology (1980): the valence × arousal map. Positions here are typical placements, not a single dataset.
  • Paul Ekman & Wallace Friesen, Facial Action Coding System (1978); Ekman, Levenson & Friesen, Science (1983) for heart rate and hand temperature.

And the rest

  • Lauri Nummenmaa et al., "Bodily maps of emotions", PNAS (2014): the body heat-maps, redrawn here by region.
  • Philippe Verduyn & Saskia Lavrijsen, "Which emotions last longest and why", Motivation and Emotion (2015): median episode lengths.
  • Brain hubs and chemistry are the textbook consensus; every emotion recruits far more than one region.
