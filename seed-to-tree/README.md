# Becoming — from seed to tree

A self-contained browser animation, drawn entirely with code. No packages, images, fonts, or network requests are needed.

Watch a seed become a leafy tree over 26 seconds. The camera starts close on the seed as it swells with water, cracks along its seam and sends out roots. The shoot pushes up through the soil bent double, scattering crumbs of earth as it breaks the surface, then straightens in the light and opens its two seed leaves. The view pulls back as the stem grows on above them, the first true leaves unfurl from tight rolls, and the woody branches spread; the seed leaves yellow and drop, followed by the juvenile leaves lower down once the canopy fills in. Hazy hills and distant trees sit behind, grass sways in front, and sunlight from the upper right shades the canopy, flickers on the ground and slants through the crown. A butterfly wanders in as the leaves come, and three birds fly in to settle in the crown at the end. The breeze and the creatures keep moving once the tree is grown.

Use **Grow again**, **Pause**, the speed control, or the growth timeline to explore the sequence. Moving the pointer subtly influences the sway and the parallax of the hills and grass. Add `?t=0.17` (any value from 0 to 1) to the URL to open paused on a particular moment.

The layout adapts to smaller screens. When reduced motion is enabled, the animation starts paused with the mature tree visible; press Play or Grow again to animate it.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder with:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit http://localhost:8000.
